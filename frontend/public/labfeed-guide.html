LabFeed Guide

Publishing a Lab Note

How to share an experiment on LabFeed — and how to do it well

🧪 What is LabFeed?

LabFeed is DogeFood Lab's on-chain social feed — the place scientists post about what they're brewing. A Lab Note is a short post, optionally with a photo, that other players can like, comment on, and tip in DOGE.

📝 How to publish a Lab Note with an image

  1. Open LabFeed from the main menu, then tap the flask + button to open the composer.
  2. Write about your experiment in the text box (placeholder reads "Share your latest experiment…"). There's a character counter so you can see how much room you have left.
  3. Tap the small image icon next to the text box to attach a photo — a screenshot of a treat you brewed works great. Images must be under 2MB and in a standard format (JPG, PNG, etc.). You'll see a preview once it's attached, with an option to remove it before posting.
  4. Tap Publish. Your note goes live immediately and appears at the top of the feed.
First time posting? You'll be asked to sign one message with your wallet. This is a one-time approval that covers all future likes and comments — it's free and doesn't cost gas, it just confirms it's really you.

🛡️ Privacy & community guidance

LabFeed is public — anything you post is visible to every player. A few things to keep in mind before you publish:

✅ Good practice

  • Post your own DogeFood Lab treats, results, and moments
  • Keep photos appropriate for all ages
  • Be respectful in comments and captions
  • Use the report option if you see something that breaks these guidelines

🚫 Avoid

  • Personal details — full name, address, phone number, financial info
  • Screenshots containing someone else's private information
  • Spam, scams, or repeated promotional posting
  • Harassment, hate speech, or graphic content

A good rule of thumb: if you wouldn't want it visible to every player in the game forever, don't post it. Lab Notes are on-chain — they aren't meant to be deleted after the fact.
