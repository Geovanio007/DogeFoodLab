🎮 Game Overview

DogeFood Lab is a Web3-powered treat creation game where you become a scientist mixing cosmic ingredients to craft unique treats. Season 2 — the Reactor update — introduces 50 all-new ingredients across 5 tiers, a new rarity system, the Arena, and the road to $LAB token rewards.

Core Objectives

  • Create Treats: Mix 1–5 ingredients to craft treats with varying rarities
  • Level Up: Gain XP to unlock higher ingredient tiers
  • Compete: Climb the leaderboard and enter the 24h Arena
  • Discover: Find secret recipes for bonus rewards
  • Collect: Build your collection of Legendary and Mythic treats

🎯 Rarity System

Your treat's rarity is determined by the ingredients you mix and the tiers they belong to.

Rarity Ingredients Points XP Requirement
Starter110–205–10Any ingredient
Rare2–350–8030–501+ Rare tier ingredient
Epic3–4100–15060–1001+ Epic tier ingredient
Legendary4–5200–300120–2001+ Legendary gate ingredient
Mythic5500–1000250–5001+ Mythic ingredient

Rarity Boosting Tips

  • Legendary Gate: Any ingredient from S2_031–S2_040 unlocks Legendary potential
  • Mythic Gate: Any ingredient from S2_041–S2_050 unlocks Mythic potential
  • More ingredients = higher rarity roll — always try to use 5
  • Tier mixing: Higher average tier across your 5 ingredients = better base roll

  • +30% Legendary treats — maximum bonus — save your best combos!

📊 Points & XP

How Points Work

  • Points are earned every time you create a treat
  • Higher rarity = more points per treat
  • Arena entry costs 50 pts but top finishes return far more
  • Heat Events apply a temporary multiplier to all point gains
  • Kernel of Wow adds a flat % bonus on top of the base value
  • Points will convert to $LAB tokens at Season 2 end

How XP Works

  • XP is earned alongside points on every treat
  • Level up to unlock higher-tier ingredients
  • Max level is 50 (unlocks Godtier Shiba Serum)
  • XP does not reset between seasons — your progress carries forward
